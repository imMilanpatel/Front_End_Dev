Our Story

A Bite of Mumbai, Right Here

Born from a grandmother's kitchen in Kutch, our dabeli recipe has been perfected over generations. We brought the authentic taste of Mumbai's street food culture — the tangy tamarind, the fiery garlic chutney, the freshness of pomegranate and the crunch of sev — all packed into a soft pav.

Every dabeli at Dabeli Darbar is made fresh to order, using our signature masala blend that we've never compromised on. No shortcuts. Only flavour.
